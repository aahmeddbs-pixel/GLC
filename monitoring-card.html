Secondary Progress MonitoringDaily lesson card — Ready · Responsible · Respectful

Student targets & instructions
1. READY — Arrive to all lessons on time with the correct equipment.
2. RESPONSIBLE — Engaged in learning with all tasks completed.
3. RESPECTFUL — Respectful communication to teachers and peers and not disturbing the learning.

Instructions to students

  • This progress monitoring card is your responsibility.
  • This card must be handed to each subject teacher at the beginning of each lesson and collected at the end.
  • Your target is 30 or more lessons each week with all three targets met.
  • Bring the card to homeroom every morning (7:50–8:10).
  • Your Grade Coordinator reviews the card at the end of each week.

Instructions to teachers

  • Ensure the student gives you their card at the beginning of each lesson and return it at the end.
  • Open today's tab and find your period. Homeroom teachers complete the Homeroom box (7:50–8:10) at the top of each day.
  • Record the arrival time (tap Now).
  • Mark each target achieved with ✓ and not achieved with ✗, then add your initials.
  • If the student leaves for the toilet, tick Toilet visit and record the time out and back in.
  • Everything saves automatically — look for Saved ✓ at the top.
